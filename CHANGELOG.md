# Changelog

## v4.0.0-beta.1

A beta under its own name, **Speedrun Sheet Beta**, so that the released mod is not updated to it. Remove Speedrun Sheet from `Mods` before installing it: with both there, the beta does not start and says so in Mod Options. Its settings start fresh; your sheet URL and the standards are kept.

- **Nothing to select.** The Chapter and Checkpoint sliders, Category, Auto-Detect, Show Selection and their hotkeys are gone, and so is the greyed row under the timer. srs times every segment that can be running. Of the old hotkeys, one remains: Open Sheet Export. Settings files lose the removed keys at the next save.
- **Chained practice.** Running several checkpoints in a row records each segment with its own time and tier. The rows above the timer show the last one completed, the most specific when several complete together: a row that asks for more than the plain one always wins over it.
- **Segments are timed with the chapter time**, so records and the export no longer depend on the room timer's type.
- **A segment is recorded from the room before it.** Walk into its first room from the room before it and it is recorded, however you reached that room: a chain, a savestate, the debug map, `console load` or Speedrun Tool's room teleport. Any other way in records nothing: 4A's Cliff Face counts only from `c-08`, and 5A's Rescue only from `d-20`.
- **A cutscene that plays as you enter a segment's first room counts in that segment**, skipped or watched: it is chapter time. 2A's `Intervention`, 3A's `Presidential Suite` and 6A's `Lake` start on the room entry, where they waited for control, so a chain of checkpoints adds up to the chapter. A wake-up still does not count: the sheet times it apart.
- **A savestate load or a room teleport starts a new attempt.** The segment you were in is not recorded, and a checkpoint crossed with the mod switched off leaves the next segment unrecorded rather than mistimed.
- **A segment CelesteTAS played any frame of is not recorded**, even when you finish it by hand: it is not shown above the timer or on the export screen, and it is never exported. That covers a TAS that stops mid-segment and hands control back, such as a quicksave replayed from a room's start. The segments after it record as usual once the TAS has stopped.
- **A Save and Quit no longer ends the attempt.** Save and Quit, pick Continue, and every segment that was running carries on, with the half second the game counts while the screen wipes. A segment that starts where you reappear, and was not running, starts there. Anything else in between is a new attempt, switching the mod off included.
- **The rows above the timer survive a chapter's end.** Finish a chapter, pick one of its checkpoints on the map, and the rows of the run you just finished stay up until you leave that checkpoint's first room (7A's do not: its credits dismiss them). Returning to the map from the pause menu during the ending keeps them too. Starting the chapter over clears them.
- **The rows whose route returns to the map are covered.** Twelve more rows: 1A's `ARB Start to RTM`, `ARB Crossing to RTM`, `ARB Heart to RTM`, `ARB Crossing-2` and `ARB Chasm-2`, with `FC Heart to RTM`, `FC Crossing to RTM` and `FC Chasm-2`; 4A's `ARB Old Trail (to RTM)`; 5A's `ARB Start`; 7A's `ARB 2500m-1` and `ARB 2500m-2`. A row that ends on a Return to Map is timed until the screen has wiped, and its berries must be collected by then: one still following you is lost with the level and does not count, so pausing with a berry in tow loses the row. `ARB 2500m-1` ends on entering 3000m like `ARB 2500m-full`; a run that takes every berry of 2500m records both and shows the full one.
- **`Heart to RTM` starts where a Save and Quit puts you back after 1A's heart.** It starts only on the spawn by the heart, so a Save and Quit taken before you cross it does not start the row. It also starts from a savestate made on that spot before you moved. A room teleport, `console load`, a death and walking into the room do not start it.
- **The chapter rows whose route returns to the map are covered.** Five more rows: `1arb IL`, `1afc IL`, `5arb IL`, `5afc IL` and `6afc IL`. With `4arb IL`, `4afc IL` and `7arb IL`, which lost a run played that way, they now carry on across a Return to Map: start at the chapter's start, return to the map, pick a checkpoint of that chapter you already reached in the run, and the row goes on. Its time is the chapter time of every piece added up, and what you collect adds up too. It ends with the chapter once everything it asks for is collected; if you finish the chapter with something left to fetch, it ends on the Return to Map that finds everything collected. 7A's credits and the Epilogue they lead to do not break the row: finish 7A one berry short, skip or watch them, return to the map, pick 2500m, fetch the berry and return to the map. A piece started from the map has to bank something the row still lacked (a berry, the heart, the cassette) before its Return to Map, or before its chapter end once the row had completed: if it brings nothing, the row is dropped, so tries at one piece do not add up into a chapter time. Dying in a checkpoint's first room right after the map resets the game's chapter time; the row keeps the time played before that death. Starting the chapter over, a checkpoint further on than the run went, or another chapter start a new attempt.
- **SomeSplitButtons' split buttons count as the game's own actions**, with a version of that mod that reports its splits to other mods. Its Save and Quit followed by the reload is a Save and Quit followed by Continue; one that reloads nothing changes nothing. Its Return to Map is a Return to Map, and a checkpoint picked from its list, or from the list Skip Cutscene opens at a chapter's end, carries a chapter row on as one picked on the map does. A list closed without a pick is a new attempt: a chapter row in progress is dropped. Without the mod, or with an older version, a split's reload is a new attempt.
- **A chapter's first segment, and a segment after a wake-up, start from a savestate made before you moved.** A single-frame tap refuses the state, and so does walking away and back. A state saved during a death, a respawn, a wake-up or an intro waits for you to appear. Entering a chapter starts its first segment only on the chapter's own spawn.
- **Variants are told apart by what you did.** The heart and cassette rows count when the run collected them, and Farewell's `DTS` rows by the dashes you have left.
- **`Start Heart RC` ends at Restart Chapter**, menu and wipe included. A restart without the heart, or after walking on into Intervention, records nothing.
- **A chapter's last segment counts the frame that stops the timer**, as the game's chapter time does. Where the timer stops during that frame (8A's `HotM Horizontal` for one), the segment is 0.017 s longer than before and than Speedrun Tool's timer shows, and it ends on the same frame as the chapter's IL. `Granny` is unchanged.
- **`Granny` is compared as file time**, 1.598 s more than the timer shows, the way its standards are timed.
- **A time of an hour or more reads `1:05:03.250`**, above the timer and on the export screen. It was shown an hour short. The export screen's delta reads as a time too (`-1:13.500`), where it counted seconds.
- **Four segments are imported that were not:** `Unravelling` (5A), `Through the Mirror` (5B) and `Stubbornness` (Farewell), now spelled as the sheet spells them, and 2A's `Start Heart RC`.
- **Export your times to your own practice sheet.** The *Open Sheet Export* hotkey, or the button of the same name in Mod Options during a level, opens a screen with your best of every segment you ran this session, in every chapter, next to the time your sheet holds, with the delta. A best is replaced only by a faster run of that same row. Nothing is written before you confirm, a row starts ticked only when it improves on your sheet, and one export sends every row you tick. The side you are playing is listed first. The same hotkey closes the screen. Setting it up: `AppsScript/SETUP.md`. The script's `/exec` URL is pasted from the clipboard, kept in `Saves/srs/export-url.txt` rather than in the settings file people share when asking for help, and never displayed or logged.
- **It never writes over a time it did not expect.** If the cell changed since srs read it, the row comes back as *sheet changed* and nothing is written. A cell srs cannot read is shown as it stands, with a `?` for the delta, and starts unticked. srs writes only into the entry tabs your sheet's Config names. Setting a Timing cell makes the sheet's own selector sync update the cells tied to it, which can sit on other tabs, when Config's *Sync selectors* allows it.
- **The screen opens at once, even offline.** srs keeps a copy of your sheet's times in `Saves/srs/sheet-times.json` and compares against it until your sheet answers, then refreshes the table, keeping the rows you ticked or unticked. The line under the title says how old the copy is and whether your sheet answered. After an export that got no answer, Export stays off until srs has read your sheet again.
- **An export sets the Timing cells.** A row's Timing cell is set as the run was played (`S&Q` / `No S&Q`), to `IL` on the all red berries and full clear chapter rows, and to `SRT` on the A-side `RTM` rows. It is left alone when srs cannot tell. Details: README.
- **A `FileTime` entry is compared fairly.** srs converts it with the sheet's buffer, shows the result in a new ADJUSTED column, and uses it for the `PB` above the timer.
- **`1arb IL` and `1afc IL` have their tier** once srs has read your sheet.
- **Auto-export on a PB (optional, off by default).** *Auto Export on PB*, in Mod Options once your web app URL is set, sends a time to your sheet as soon as its segment ends, with no screen. It sends every row the export screen would tick by itself, under the same rules, Timing cells included. It never sends a row your sheet does not have, and a time it wrote is not written again if you then change the cell. When every row it sent is written, a popup says so; *Popup When Sent*, under it, turns that off. If a row is refused or your sheet does not answer, one message says how many rows were not written; they stay ticked on the export screen, and a request that got no answer goes again with your next improved segment. While the setting is on, unticking a row on the export screen does not hold it back. It waits while the export screen is open. The sending is done off the game's thread.
- **Nothing is recorded under an assist or a variant.** Game speed, invincibility, extra dashes, Mirror Mode and the others switch recording off while they are on, as the *Enabled* switch does; Play as Badeline does not. Such a time showed its tier and could be exported.
- **Mod Options is grouped.** With the mod on, the section is five rows when closed: *Enabled*, *Display* (the switches for the rows above the timer), *Update Standards*, *Your Sheet* (*Open Sheet Export*, *Auto Export on PB*, *Popup When Sent*, the web app URL and *Forget Sheet Web App URL*) and *Keybinds*.
- **Selecting *Update Standards* shows when the standards were last updated**, in place of a segment count. When an update fails, at launch too, the line stays up in red and says which standards are still in use, until an update goes through.
- **Show PB Improvement says what it needs.** Selecting it shows a line: the PB is the time your sheet holds, so the switch does nothing until a sheet web app URL is set.
- **A hotkey can explain itself.** *Cycle Shown Segment* shows a line under its row on the Keybinds screen saying what it does.
- **The Toggle Tier Display hotkey is gone.** *Display* holds five switches, and a hotkey for one of them no longer made sense. *Show Tier* stays in Mod Options. Settings files lose the binding at the next save.
- **The URL entries say which URL.** They read *Set Sheet Web App URL from clipboard*, *Replace Sheet Web App URL from clipboard* and *Forget Sheet Web App URL*: it is the web app's address that srs needs, not the sheet's. While none is set, the line under the entry says what to copy and where to find out how to get it. Once one is set, selecting the entry shows the day it was set.
- **A sheet that stops answering shows in Mod Options.** When the last read of your sheet failed, the line under the URL entry turns red and gives the reason, until a read goes through. It showed only on the export screen and in the auto-export message.
- **The export screen lists what beats your sheet first.** The rows a session best improves come first, then the others, each part in the order the screen had: the side you are playing, then the sheet's order. The chapter header comes again where the second part starts. Ticking or unticking a row does not move it.
- **Long lines on the export screens wrap.** A message or a summary line too wide for the screen was cut with `...`; it now continues on the next line.
- **An out-of-date script shows in Mod Options.** While your sheet's script is out of date, the line under the web app URL entry says so in red, until srs has read the current script. Nothing is exported in that state, by hand or automatically.
- **srs needs the current export script.** If your copy's script is older, the screen and the URL check say so, and Export stays off until srs has read the current one: copy the script again from the template and publish a new version of the deployment. A script newer than srs asks you to update srs instead. An export is sent 25 rows at a time, and the screen counts the rows answered: a session of 200 rows takes about two minutes, more when many rows set a Timing cell. If a request gets no answer, the rows already answered are kept. The summary groups the rows written and those already on the sheet.
- **The reference times are called *standards*.** *Update Sheet Data* is now **Update Standards**, and switching the mod back on runs it.
- **The reference sheet moved, and srs follows it.** srs reads the new document, and repoints your settings on launch if they still name the old one. A tab you pointed elsewhere on purpose is left alone.
- **The Keybinds screen is the one AxiomeVI's other mods share, and records a combo in one pass.** Press Confirm on a row, hold the whole combo (up to four keys or buttons) and let go. A key already held when recording starts counts only once pressed again, and **Journal** or **Delete** clears a row. With `T` and `Shift+T` bound to two hotkeys, `Shift+T` fires only its own. Hotkeys stay quiet while the window is unfocused or another mod's Keybinds screen is open. Two hotkeys on the same combo are drawn red, stick directions can be bound, and Escape cannot. The *Clear all bindings* button is gone.
- **No warning in `log.txt` on srs's first launch.** It said the temporary export URL file could not be deleted, when its folder did not exist yet.
- **A file or settings write that fails no longer crashes the game.** Setting or forgetting the web app URL says when the file could not be written or deleted.
- **Standards that cannot be saved to disk are still used for the session**, and the files on disk stay those of one download.
- **The URL check repeats the export script's own error** when the script answers with one, where it said that something else had answered.
- **The tier row moved above Speedrun Tool's timer, and became two rows**: the segment srs detected (`1a Crossing`), over your time and tier in the tier's color, then `PB` and your gain in gold (`PB -0.214`) when your own sheet holds a slower time, then the gap to the next tier in red (`+0.140 to Purple 1`). The rows show from the moment a segment completes until you leave the room or load a savestate, while that timer is on, and no longer make room for srta's row.
- **Each part has its switch** in Mod Options, all on by default: **Show Checkpoint Name**, **Show Time**, **Show Tier**, **Show PB Improvement** and **Show Delta to Next Tier**. **Show Tier** and its hotkey now hide only the tier's name: the time stays, in the tier's color.
- **A tier needs a time strictly under its threshold**, as on the sheet. `Gold` is the best tier: the WR column no longer ranks, so a time under the WR reads `Gold` and shows no gap. The time is ranked to the millisecond you see.
- **Tier colours follow the sheet's current palette.** Each tier is drawn in its cell colour on the sheet; Gold is drawn in its gold text colour, Unranked in grey.
- **A hotkey to look back over a chain.** Cycle Shown Segment, unbound by default, steps the rows above the timer back through the segments of the attempt, and wraps to the latest. `2/3` before the name says which one is shown. The attempt goes on through a Save and Quit and Continue, and through the map while a chapter row carries on: at the end of a chapter run in pieces, every piece can be stepped back to.
- **The background of the rows above the timer follows the text.** It ran far past a long row; it now fades out the same short distance after every row.
- **Replacing the web app URL asks first.** Press once and the button asks, press again to replace. Setting the first URL, or pasting the same one again, does not ask.
- **Three more standards tabs are downloaded:** the C-side, all red berries and full clear standards. All three are used. An update still succeeds only if every tab downloads, and the log now names the tab that failed.
- **The all red berries rows are covered.** 36 more rows, named `ARB` under their chapter (`1arb Start`, `7arb IL`): the checkpoints of 1A to 5A, 7A and 8A, and the ILs of 2A, 3A, 4A, 7A and 8A. A berry row counts when the run collected every red berry it asks for; one still following you at the end counts, a golden never does. Some rows also need the heart, and the two `to Heart` rows end on it. In 4A the berry route enters Cliff Face from `c-10` and the plain one from `c-08`: each row counts only on its own. On the export screen they sit under their chapter, below a header of their own (`1arb`). `Wake Up` is left out.
- **7A's 3000m is also timed in its three pieces**, as the sheet does: `Downdraft` from the checkpoint to the end of the second room, `Updraft` for the third room, `Nodraft` from the fourth to the summit, each with its `ARB` row. A piece starts when you walk into its first room, and `3000m` is still timed whole across them.
- **The full clear rows are covered.** 22 rows, named `FC` under their chapter (`2afc Start`, `7afc IL`): the checkpoints where the full clear route differs from the berry one, and the chapters 2A, 3A, 4A, 7A, 8A and Farewell (`FC DTS IL`, `FC No DTS IL`) in one piece. A row counts when the run collected what the full clear route collects there: its red berries, and a cassette, a heart or a summit gem; Farewell's rows ask for the moon berry. 2A's take no heart, which has a row of its own. 5A's `FC Depths` and 6A's `FC Hollows (from RTM)` start only from their checkpoint's own spawn, and the second asks for nothing. 8A's is `HotM Horizontal Tape`, typed on the A-side tab. Most of these rows have no tier on the sheet yet and read `Unranked`.
- **7A's summit gem rows are covered**: `Start Gem`, `500m Gem`, `1000m Gem`, `1500m Gem Tape`, `2000m Gem` and `2500m Gem`, each its checkpoint with the gem collected on the way.
- **Five berry rows start only from their checkpoint's own spawn**, picked on the map or from a savestate made there before you moved, never by walking in: 1A's `ARB Crossing to Heart`, `ARB Crossing` and `ARB Chasm`, 4A's `ARB Cliff Face (from RTM)` and 5A's `ARB Depths`.
- **B-sides are covered.** Every B-side now has its checkpoint rows and its whole-chapter row, named `IL` (`5b IL` and `6b IL` in the chapters that hold both sides): 33 more rows. A B-side `IL` starts where its chapter's `Start` does and ends when the heart stops the timer. `7b Start` and `7b IL` are timed as their 7A rows are, from the landing, with the same 5.508 s added.
- **The A-side heart and cassette rows are covered.** 37 more rows are imported, on checkpoints and on ILs: `Chasm Tape Clear`, `Shrine Heart RTM`, `IL Tape Clear`, `IL Heart Tape RTM` and the like. An `RTM` row ends at the collect, the later one when the row marks both. - **An export that got no answer in time waits for the sheet.** The script may still be writing, so the export screen says so and keeps Export off for three minutes, the longest the script can still run, then reads the sheet again and offers what is left. Auto-export waits the same way.
- **Three rows are renamed**, since each now has a twin: `Depths Tape` is `Depths Tape RTM`, `Hollows Tape` is `Hollows Tape RTM` and `Shrine Heart` is `Shrine Heart Clear`. Their rows on the sheet are the same.

## v3.7.0 — 2026-08-27 by Axiome

- **Master switch**: a new **Enabled** toggle at the top of Mod Options turns the whole mod off — no tier row, no selection row, no auto-detection, no hotkey and no sheet download on launch. While it is off the section shows nothing but that toggle. Nothing is discarded: switching back on resumes on the same checkpoint and category, and refreshes the sheet on the spot in case it was retimed meanwhile.
- **Hotkeys are combos, and have their own screen**: the three rebindable hotkeys (Switch Category, Toggle Tier Display, Toggle Selection Display) now fire only when *all* the keys bound to them are held together — `Ctrl + C` rather than "Ctrl or C" — and they are bound from **Keybinds**, a new screen inside the mod's own section, with a keyboard list and a controller list. Press a key to add it to a combo, press it again to remove it, **Delete** clears a row and the button at the bottom clears everything. They no longer show up in Everest's key config screen, which has no way to express a combo. Bindings already set there are kept, and a single-key one behaves exactly as before; a binding holding two keys, however, used to mean either of them and is now read as both at once, so rebind those from the new screen.
- **`Keys.None` is cleaned out of the bindings on launch**: pressing an unmappable key while rebinding — AZERTY's `)` is one — used to bind an FNA placeholder that the game reports as held for *every* such key, so the hotkey fired on keys you never bound. Any binding still carrying it is stripped at startup, and the new screen refuses it. F1, F2, F3 and F5 are refused too: they are Everest's debug keys, and a mod misbehaving is exactly when you need them.

## v3.2.0 — 2026-08-06

- **Segments the sheet does not time from their checkpoint**: two any% segments start somewhere else than the in-game checkpoint they belong to, and both were mistimed. **2A Awake** is timed from the moment Madeline wakes up — room `end_0`, the campfire right after the dream section — which is *three* rooms before the game's Awake checkpoint; it now starts there, and a run of **Intervention** ends there too instead of overrunning into Awake's first three rooms. **7A Start** is timed from `a-00`: neither the intro room nor Madeline's landing animation counts, their time being added on the sheet afterwards (run it the usual way — savestate placed after the landing, **Current Room** timer). Auto-detection follows: entering one of those rooms selects the segment, without waiting for the checkpoint.
- **7A Start gets its untimed head added back**: the 5.508s of the intro room and the landing animation are part of what the sheet's 7A Start thresholds describe, so srs adds them to the captured time. Without it a 7A Start run was compared against thresholds covering more than it had timed, and came out several tiers too high. The greyed, tierless time of a run that did not start at `a-00` is left exactly as the timer showed it.
- **The captured time was one frame too long**: srs read the room timer *after* the frame it ends on had been added to it, while SpeedrunTool freezes its own display just before. Every result was one frame slower than the timer above it — and than the sheet's references, which are recorded with SpeedrunTool. srs now reads the same value SpeedrunTool freezes.
- **"Cassette" is now "Any% Cassettes"**: the 5A and 6A cassettes are part of the any% run, so the category says which run its segments belong to. The rule behind the naming: a category never holds two segments starting at the same in-game checkpoint, and wider categories are described by what they add to a smaller one.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-07-18

## v0.3.0 — 2026-07-18
